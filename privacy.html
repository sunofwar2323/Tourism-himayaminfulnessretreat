Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers the website of Himalaya Mindfulness Retreat Tours & Travels, a tour operator based in Thimphu, Bhutan (business licence no. 06013165).

Who is responsible for your information

Himalaya Mindfulness Retreat Tours & Travels, 11011 Thimphu, Bhutan. For any privacy question or request, email himalayamindfulnessretreat@gmail.com or call +975 17890334.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through an enquiry form or by email:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Anything else you add: phone or WhatsApp number, travel dates or month, number of travellers, the journey or places you are interested in, and your message.

Please don’t send passport numbers, payment details or health information through the website forms. If a booking needs them, we will ask for them separately.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and plan a journey with you.
  • To prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, arrange your trip.

We don’t sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Services that handle your information

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes website enquiries to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts the email inbox where enquiries arrive.
  • Vercel hosts this website. Like any web host, it keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the service.

These providers may store data outside Bhutan. Each handles it under its own privacy terms.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The travel guide page remembers your light or dark display choice in your browser’s local storage; it never leaves your device. Fonts, scripts and photos are served from this website, not from third-party servers.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to answer you and to plan and support your journey, and for any period we are legally required to keep business records. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you have sent us. Email himalayamindfulnessretreat@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we can.

Children

This website is meant for adults planning travel. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us on their behalf.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the website handles information, we will update this page and the date above.
