Booking Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

These conditions apply to journeys arranged by Himalaya Mindfulness Retreat Tours & Travels, a tour operator based in Thimphu, Bhutan (business licence no. 06013165). A booking is confirmed only when you accept a written quote from us. That quote, together with this policy, sets the terms of your trip.

Passports and visas

You must carry a valid passport and have obtained all of the appropriate visas, permits and certificates for the countries you will visit before or after your Bhutan trip. Your passport must be valid for six months beyond the duration of the trip. It is your responsibility to ensure that you are in possession of the correct visas, permits and certificates for your trip.

Deposit and payment

You will be required to pay a deposit or make full payment upon confirmation of your booking. The remaining balance can be settled up to three weeks prior to arrival, or upon arrival. For tours that include luxury hotels, full payment may be required, and we will provide this information before confirming your booking.

Himalaya Mindfulness Retreat Tours & Travels guarantees that the price of your tour will not increase after confirmation.

Airfare and flight confirmation

Our tour cost does not include airfare. If you need assistance with international or domestic flights, we can help arrange them. We strongly recommend that you reconfirm flight times with the airline before departure.

For a smooth departure, check in at least three hours before both domestic and international flights, and be at the gate at least 45 minutes prior to departure.

Young travellers

Travellers under 18 years old must be accompanied by an adult guardian throughout the entire trip. The accompanying adult is fully responsible for the minor’s behaviour, well-being and supervision, including ensuring the minor is adequately monitored and cared for during the journey.

Each adult accompanying one or more minors accepts full accountability for these matters and agrees to follow all related terms and conditions.

Emergency medical treatment

In a medical emergency during your trip, we will promptly take you to the nearest hospital or medical facility for necessary treatment. Our team will make every effort to see that you receive appropriate care and additional support as needed. Your well-being is our priority, and we will coordinate with medical professionals so you receive the best care available.

Insurance

The cost of the tour does not include insurance. We strongly recommend that every guest obtain comprehensive cover for trip cancellation due to illness, accident or injury, personal accident, personal liability, and loss of or damage to baggage. We are not responsible or liable for issues arising from a lack of adequate insurance. Your policy should also cover emergencies that may occur during the trip.

Responsibility and limitation of liability

Travel can involve risks outside our control. These include travel through less developed areas, transport by car, plane or horseback, natural forces, political instability, and accident, illness, damage, loss, delay or irregularity, including in remote places where quick evacuation or medical assistance may not be readily available.

We take steps to prioritise your safety and comfort. We cannot be held responsible for risks or dangers that are outside our control. If a tour is cancelled because of circumstances we cannot control, such as weather, transport strikes, political unrest or war, guests bear any cost incurred in the course of the tour.

Images and marketing

During a tour we may take photographs or video that include you, subject to your approval. With your consent, these may be used for marketing, promotion and related materials. We respect your privacy. If you agree to license those images or videos to us, they will be used only for these purposes, across the mediums we use, without further obligation or compensation to you.
